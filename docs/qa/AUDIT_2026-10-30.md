## 5. Security

Re-checked on today's code: every server action has a permission check (148/148; sign-up, forgot and reset password are
public by design, with per-IP and per-email limits, a signed 30-minute one-time reset token, and answers that never reveal
whether an account exists). Cross-business isolation 14/14. Webhooks are signature-checked (Meta, Razorpay). The plan lock is
enforced on the server, not only in the menu. Stored XSS 22/22. Production data was not read or changed.
