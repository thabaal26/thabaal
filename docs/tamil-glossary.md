# Thabaal Tamil Glossary (தமிழ் சொல்லகராதி)

This glossary defines the standard, everyday Tamil vocabulary used throughout the Thabaal WhatsApp portal (`www.thabaal.in`). All UI labels, buttons, navigation items, stat cards, empty states, and notifications follow these terms to ensure natural, consistent, and respectful communication for shop owners and retail staff in Tamil Nadu (e.g., Thenan Oils, Madurai).

---

## 1. Protected Terms (ஆங்கிலத்திலேயே வைக்க வேண்டிய சொற்கள்)
The following terms remain in standard English script across all screens and never undergo translation or transliteration:
- **WhatsApp**
- **UPI**
- **GST**
- **OTP**
- **Meta**
- **PDF**
- **Excel**
- **Google Sheets**

## 6. Grammar & Locale Rules (மொழி இலக்கணம் மற்றும் வடிவமைப்பு)
1. **Natural Everyday Tamil**: Always prioritize simple, polite conversational Tamil used in shops and markets. Never use formal archaic literary Tamil (no "ஈண்டு", "ஆயினும்").
2. **Numbers & Currencies**: All numerals, phone numbers, and rupee figures stay in normal digits (e.g., `₹280`, `+91 98765 43210`).
3. **Natural Plurals**: Sentences with numbers must read naturally without parentheses:
   - Correct: `3 ஆர்டர்கள்`, `5 வாடிக்கையாளர்கள்`
   - Incorrect: `3 ஆர்டர்(கள்)`, `5 வாடிக்கையாளர்(கள்)`
4. **Dates and Times**: Formatted using the `"ta-IN"` locale (e.g. `17 அக்டோபர் 2026`).
5. **No Layout Breaking**: Tamil words are 30–50% longer than English. Never clip key actions with ellipsis (`...`). Ensure generous flex wrapping and minimum 40px tap targets on mobile.
